# M12 — Learning analytics, readiness, and performance intelligence

Date: 2026-08-14. Builds on M11 (`381eccb`).

Scope note: M12 turns the data M6–M11 already store into actionable
intelligence. The core principle of the instruction is absolute and
shaped every decision below: M12 IS NOT A SECOND MASTERY ENGINE. M8
remains the sole authority for mastery computation, review urgency, study
priority, spaced repetition, and recommendations — analytics CONSUMES
those outputs and never re-derives them. Everything is deterministic,
LLM-free, and computed on read from the student's own rows.

## What shipped

### The pure analytics read model (spec A/B — ADR-0033)

`packages/analytics` is a new pure package with one conceptual entry
point: `getCourseAnalytics(input) → CourseAnalytics`. The app fetches
bounded owner-readable rows, hands them over, and renders the result
verbatim — the UI computes no metric (spec A). No new tables, no stored
analytics state, no duplicate datasets (spec B): mastery states come from
M8's `masteryState`, the misconception signal reuses M8's own threshold
constant, and the exam-focus list IS `rankConcepts` output sliced to
three (spec S — no second priority algorithm).

### Centralized evidence thresholds (spec AJ)

Every minimum-evidence gate lives in `thresholds.ts`, documented and
stamped `ANALYTICS_RULES_VERSION = 1`: trend minimums (5 attempts per
window, ±15-point delta), per-concept evidence floors (3 attempts before
any attention flag; 4 before a strength), category and calibration
floors, readiness coverage/strength gates, simulation sample sizes, and
display caps (5 attention items, 3 insights). Below a floor, sections say
"not enough data yet" — never NaN, never a fake 0%.

### Time windows in the student's timezone (spec C/AR)

`windows.ts` computes today / last-7 / last-30 / course-to-date and the
week-over-week split as CALENDAR days in the student's timezone using the
same domain helpers as the countdown UIs, with DST-crossing tests
(spring-forward and fall-back in America/New_York). The per-attempt
calendar-age lookup is memoized behind a bounded cache after the
performance test caught Intl formatting costing ~4.5s over 5,000 attempts
(spec AV) — pure memoization, determinism unchanged.

### Mastery map, needs-attention, strengths (spec E/F/H/I)

The distribution counts concepts across the five M8 states with an
explicit assessed-coverage figure. Needs-attention is evidence-backed and
honest: a concept is flagged only with ≥3 attempts, every flag carries
coded reasons (misconception signal, high-confidence errors, low mastery,
declining trend, due for review) ranked worst-first and capped at five —
and UNASSESSED IS NEVER CALLED WEAK. Strengths require a strong state
plus ≥4 attempts and no active attention reasons. Per-concept analytics
add recency, per-cognitive-level accuracy, and a deterministic trend.

### Deterministic trends (spec G)

`classifyTrend` compares two windows and refuses to judge until both hold
≥5 attempts; within evidence, a ±15-point accuracy delta separates
improving/stable/declining. One question can never flip a verdict.

### Cognitive, difficulty, and calibration analytics (spec J/K/L)

Accuracy by the five cognitive levels and three difficulties (null below
5 attempts each). Confidence calibration buckets tagged answers into
calibrated-confident / overconfident / underconfident / aware-gap,
requires 10 tagged attempts, surfaces an overconfidence signal only at ≥2
certain-but-wrong answers, and the UI copy is supportive by design
("that's a normal part of learning"), never scolding.

### Exam readiness — a state, never a grade (spec N–R)

Readiness is one of EARLY / BUILDING / ON TRACK / STRONG with WHY reason
codes. Coverage is distinct from mastery (spec P): a student acing 30% of
concepts is BUILDING with "coverage is low" plus an honest "what's
assessed looks solid" — high mastery share cannot leap the coverage
gates. Under 20 attempts the whole card carries a low-confidence caveat
(spec Q). The focus list is M8's ranking, and the screen states in plain
text that readiness "describes your preparation, not your grade — no
score is being predicted" (spec R). Scope note: no exam→concept mapping
exists in the schema, so readiness reads course-wide against the next
upcoming exam (documented in `readiness.ts`).

### Consistency, modes, medication, clinical judgment (spec T–Y)

Consistency derives from attempt timestamps — never session-open time
(spec U): active days in 7/30, the M10 non-punitive streak rule, a
7-day daily-attempt strip, and completed/abandoned session counts, with
no minutes→mastery causal claims anywhere (spec V). Mode analytics gate
accuracy at 5 attempts per mode; medication analytics cover
medication-typed concepts plus numeric calculations (spec X). Clinical
judgment shows analysis questions, prioritization questions, and
simulation CJMM dimensions SIDE BY SIDE — deliberately no blended score,
because they measure different things (spec Y).

### Simulation analytics via one compact RPC (spec Z/AA/AK/AO)

`simulation_sessions.state/score/definition` are server-only, so
migration 0013 adds read-only SECURITY DEFINER
`get_simulation_analytics(course_id)`: per-completed-session aggregates —
case key/title, outcome kind/label resolved from the session's pinned
definition SNAPSHOT (a reseed cannot relabel history), duration,
earned/possible, per-dimension points, and missed-critical/unsafe COUNTS.
The labeled lists and hidden case internals stay debrief-only. Same
ownership gate as every simulation RPC; revoked from anon. The engine
aggregates outcomes, dimension shares (gated at 4 possible points),
weakest dimension, and an early-half/late-half score trend that stays
"insufficient" below 3 completed sessions (spec AA). Hint-usage analytics
are documented as unavailable (hints are client-only events).

### Error patterns and insights (spec AB/AC/AD)

Four deterministic error patterns (high-confidence misses,
prioritization-clustered misses, hard-difficulty-clustered misses,
repeated same-concept misses), each requiring ≥3 backing errors and the
cluster patterns requiring a majority of misses. Insights are an ordered
deterministic rule list capped at three, each with a CTA into an EXISTING
engine: review a flagged concept, start an adaptive session (M8/M9
scheduling), open Who First? (M10), or run a simulation (M11). No new
"insight engine" and no LLM anywhere (spec AN).

### The analytics UI (spec AE–AI)

`/course/[id]/analytics` renders the read model as restrained cards:
insights first, then readiness, mastery map (accessible bars with text
alternatives), needs-attention (tappable into concept pages), strengths,
consistency (with an accessibility-labeled 7-day strip), week-over-week,
cognitive/difficulty tables, confidence check, modes and medication,
clinical judgment, simulations, and error patterns. Whole percents only —
no decimals (spec E). Mobile stacks one column; ≥900px flows two columns
(spec AG/AH). The empty state is honest and encouraging with a "Start
practicing" CTA (spec AI). The course detail screen gains an "Analytics"
button, and the M1 placeholder tabs came alive: Progress and Weaknesses
now open a course chooser into the analytics page (nav placeholder flags
corrected — every destination is live).

### Privacy (spec AM)

Nothing analytics touches reaches an AI provider or third-party
transport. Exactly one payload-free event was added: `analytics_viewed` —
by construction it cannot carry a metric, mastery number, or course text.

### Testing (spec AS/AT/AU/AV)

`packages/analytics`: 13 suites / 65 tests. Golden tests pin the five
synthetic students (spec AS): A (strong recall, weak prioritization), B
(90% accuracy on 30% coverage → BUILDING with honest reasons), C
(repeated certain-but-wrong → overconfidence + misconception surfacing),
D (good simulation cue recognition, weak reassessment), E (no attempts →
fully-formed empty output, nothing invented), plus a 3-run determinism
check. Integrity tests (spec AU) fuzz ghost concepts, null concepts,
future-dated attempts, zero-possible simulations, duplicates, and empty
input through a recursive no-NaN/no-Infinity assertion. The performance
test (spec AV) runs 5,000 attempts / 200 concepts / 25 simulations under
a generous bound. App-side: 7 new UI tests against the REAL engine
(readiness + disclaimer, needs-attention evidence, insight CTA routing,
simulation aggregates, no-grade regex, empty state, error path), plus the
authz script's new check 58 (below).

### Authorization check 58 (spec AO/AP)

The live-database script completes the harness's simulation session with
a minimal valid score, then proves: the owner reads exactly their one
completed session with correct counts; the serialized response contains
no hidden finding text and no `rules`/`findings` keys; user B is refused
on A's course; anonymous clients are refused.

## Manual validation walkthrough (spec AW)

Prerequisites: a signed-in student with one course containing processed
materials, generated questions, at least one upcoming exam, and the
seeded simulation library.

1. Open a course → tap **Analytics**. With zero attempts, verify the
   honest empty state appears ("Nothing to analyze yet") with a Start
   practicing CTA — no zeros, no fake charts.
2. Tap **Start practicing** and answer 3–4 questions, then return to
   Analytics. Verify sections below their evidence floors say "not
   enough data yet" rather than showing percentages.
3. Verify the mastery map shows counts across the five M8 states and an
   "N of M concepts assessed" coverage line.
4. Verify a concept with under 3 attempts NEVER appears under Needs
   attention, even if its only answer was wrong (unassessed ≠ weak).
5. Answer several questions on one concept incorrectly (≥3 attempts).
   Verify it appears under Needs attention with a plain-language reason.
6. Tap the flagged concept row. Verify it opens that concept's page.
7. Verify Strengths stays empty until a concept is Strong with ≥4
   attempts, then shows it with a ✓.
8. Verify the "What to do next" card shows at most 3 insights and each
   button routes somewhere real (concept page, adaptive session, modes,
   simulations).
9. In "This week", verify the trend reads "Not enough data yet" until
   both weeks hold ≥5 attempts — one good day must not read "Improving".
10. Verify "How you think" shows accuracy per cognitive level only for
    levels with ≥5 attempts, as whole percents with counts.
11. Verify the difficulty rows behave the same way.
12. Answer ≥10 questions with confidence tags. Verify the Confidence
    check appears with matched-share and the four cells.
13. Miss two questions tagged "Certain". Verify the overconfidence note
    appears and reads supportively — no scolding language.
14. Verify Exam readiness names the next exam with its date in YOUR
    timezone and a day count matching the exam screen's countdown.
15. Verify the readiness state is one of Early days / Building / On
    track / Strong position, with at least one WHY line under it.
16. With under 20 total attempts, verify the "Based on limited evidence"
    caveat shows.
17. Read the whole card and confirm the "not your grade — no score is
    being predicted" line is present and no grade/percentage prediction
    appears anywhere.
18. Verify "Focus next" lists up to 3 concepts consistent with the Study
    tab's recommendations (same engine, same order).
19. In Consistency, verify the streak matches the Modes screen's streak
    and active-day counts look right; verify the 7-day strip's bars match
    the days you actually studied (device timezone).
20. Verify a session you abandoned shows in "left unfinished" and that
    no study-minutes figure appears anywhere on the page.
21. Play a Who First? mode session. Verify the Study modes card shows it
    with sessions and (once ≥5 attempts) accuracy.
22. Verify Medication questions shows accuracy only once real
    medication/calculation attempts exist.
23. Complete one simulation. Verify the Simulations card shows 1
    completed with the outcome, points earned/possible, and NO hidden
    case content (no unrevealed findings, no rule text).
24. Verify the simulation score trend reads "Not enough data yet" until
    3 simulations are complete, and Clinical judgment shows question
    accuracy and CJMM dimensions side by side with no combined score.
25. Resize the browser window across 900px. Verify cards flow into two
    columns on desktop and stack on mobile, and that bar charts carry
    text alternatives (inspect accessibility labels).
26. Open the **Progress** and **Weaknesses** tabs. Verify both list your
    courses and open this same analytics page; verify a second account
    sees NONE of this course's analytics (cross-user isolation) and a
    signed-out client cannot call `get_simulation_analytics`.

## What M12 deliberately did not do (spec AX)

No faculty-facing dashboard, no cohort or comparative analytics, no
grade prediction or pass-probability claims, no leaderboards, no export
pipelines, no third-party analytics transport, no stored/derived
analytics tables, no second mastery or priority engine — and no M13 work.
M12 stops here.
